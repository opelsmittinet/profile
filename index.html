The ROX Impact

The Struggle

An AV and Conference System company hit a significant growth blocker during the pandemic. The lack of a robust work optimization system led to chronic meeting inefficiency and a disconnect between leadership's core values and daily operational execution.

The Framework

Implemented a comprehensive redesign of organizational rhythm: Time Management re-structuring, Timeboxing KPIs, and Milestone tracking.

The Result

  • Weekly Efficiency: Reduced meeting times by 1 hour.
  • Cultural Infrastructure: Established concrete Career Paths.
  • Sustainable Growth: Transitioned core values into habits.
